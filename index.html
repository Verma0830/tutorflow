Quick Starter Guide for Mom 🌟

1

Mark Attendance: Go to the Attendance tab, select the date, and click status buttons. Sundays are automatically set to Tuition Holiday!

2

Add/Edit Students: Switch to the Students tab to add new students, edit base fees, or set their Joining Date (for mid-month fee pro-rating).

3

Fee Tracking: Go to the Fee Tracker. The system automatically subtracts Tuition Holiday days to calculate exact prorated fees to pay!

4

Excel Sync: On your laptop, you can upload your excel sheet in the Students tab, or download the updated monthly sheets anytime!
